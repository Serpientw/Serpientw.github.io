About Me

I am currently a third-year master's student in Information Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Assoc. Prof. Jiangchao Yao and Prof. Yanfeng Wang. I was a research intern with the Pailitao Algorithm team at Taotian Group (Zhejiang Tmall Technology Co., Ltd.), where I worked with Xu Chen on efficient diffusion LLM inference. Before that, I earned a B.E. in Information Engineering (IEEE Honor Class) and a B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics through the dual bachelor's degree program at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2024.

My research focuses on building efficient and reliable large language models, with an emphasis on diffusion large language models (dLLMs), efficient inference, and multimodal learning.
