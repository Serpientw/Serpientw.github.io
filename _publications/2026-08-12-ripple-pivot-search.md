We propose **Ripple-Pivot Search (RPS)**, a training-free decoding method for diffusion language models. RPS uses lookahead evaluation to identify pivot tokens whose commitment reduces uncertainty across remaining masked positions, accelerating diffusion LLM inference while preserving generation quality.
