I am a master's student in Information Engineering at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/), advised by [Assoc. Prof. Jiangchao Yao](https://sunarker.github.io/) and Prof. Yanfeng Wang. I am also a research intern with the Pailitao Algorithm team at Zhejiang Tmall Technology Co., Ltd., working with Xu Chen.

My research focuses on building efficient and reliable large language models, with an emphasis on diffusion large language models (dLLMs), efficient inference, and multimodal learning.

## Research interests

- Diffusion large language models
- Efficient and reliable LLM inference
- Multimodal learning

## Current focus

My recent work studies training-free decoding methods for dLLMs. I am interested in how token interactions, lookahead evaluation, and continuous representations can make parallel generation faster while preserving quality.
